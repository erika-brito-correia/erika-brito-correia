## 👩‍💻 About me

I'm an **AI Builder** and Process Automation Engineer at **Vasta**, based in Vitória da Conquista, Bahia 🇧🇷.
My path started in **software QA**, where I learned to look at details and chase efficiency in every step. That curiosity led me to automation and AI: today I design agents, integrations and data pipelines that take real work off people's hands, from sales conversations to support and reporting.

I'm finishing my Electrical Engineering degree (Control & Automation) at **IFBA**, and I'm a community leader for women in tech and engineering.

> I believe technology doesn't replace people, it amplifies what they can do.

## 🛠️ What I build

- 🤖 **AI sales agents:** WhatsApp SDR agents that qualify leads and book meetings, with versioned prompts and failure-mode evaluation.
- 🎧 **Support automation:** AI-assisted ticket routing across multiple stores.
- 📊 **Experimentation & CRO:** daily A/B test reports (Claude + Slack approval), monthly CRO reports, Airtable → Supabase migrations, GA4 monitoring.
- 📚 **Knowledge systems:** RAG pipelines over legal corpora, with extraction, OCR and quality audits.
- 🧩 **Internal products:** client onboarding app, web calculators, a desktop video editor (Electron + FFmpeg) and a personal second brain with Obsidian + Claude Code (hooks, skills, subagents, memory).

## 🔬 Research & engineering projects

- **Coffee-leaf disease detection** (INCITE ICT, IFBA): machine learning + computer vision system designed for agricultural spraying drones, to apply treatment only where needed.
- **Autonomous wheelchair** (Catalisa ICT, with Fiocruz and INPI): startup structuring, patent landscape and commercialization strategy; plus an accessible web interface (React) to control the wheelchair.
- **Electrical installations project** for a 5-floor building (university course).
